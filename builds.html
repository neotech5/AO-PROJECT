Build Solo Terbaik

Build buat solo boss, farm fame, dan bertahan di Roads of Avalon. Skill + gear + budget.

⚠️ IP & spec kamu sama pentingnya dengan build. Target IP ≥ 1.000-1.100 buat solo boss/Ice Spider. Jangan bossing pakai set yang gak rela hilang.

🐺 Bloodmoon Staff — Solo Boss

⭐ TERBAIK ALL-ROUND

Senjata Two-Handed (Shapeshifter). Sustain lifesteal gila lewat Werewolf — sanggup tahan boss tanky kayak Ice Spider sambil farm fame cepat.

Skill (fix)

  • Q — Reality Fissure (AoE dmg + heal, skill utama)
  • W — Distortion (AoE DoT + slow, sustain)
  • E — Werewolf Transformation (lifesteal lawan boss)
  • Passive — Altered Beast

Gear (2H — tanpa off-hand)

  • Kepala: Stalker Hood (Mortal Agony)
  • Armor: Mercenary Jacket (Bloodlust)
  • Sepatu: Soldier Boots (Rejuvenating Sprint)
  • Cape: Undead Cape (anti-CC)
  • Pot/Food: Major Healing + Beef Stew

Rotasi: Hood (Mortal Agony) → numpuk Shift Charge pakai Q+W → E Werewolf → spam Tear Open/Rip Through (di bawah 40% HP lifesteal Bloody Roar nyala) → kena CC pakai Undead Cape, HP kritis Sprint mundur.

🐻 Bear Paws

Pemula-friendly

Bruiser sustain: burst + heal besar, gampang dipakai. Bagus kalau Bloodmoon kemahalan atau spec belum ada.

🔨 1H Mace (Hand of Justice)

Aman/Tanky

Klasik solo boss: tanky + sustain, bisa stun-lock boss. Aman tapi lebih lambat dari Bloodmoon. Pasangkan Mercenary Jacket.

🩸 Spiked Gauntlets

Sustain tinggi

Bruiser self-heal tinggi, enak buat boss HP gede. DPS stabil + survivability bagus.

👤 Shadowcaller

Buat PvP 1v1

Sama-sama Cursed Staff (spec nyambung Bloodmoon). DoT poison nyebar cepat — 'broken' buat duel/ratter di roads, bukan bossing.

💰 Tips Budget

  • Modal kecil (±2m): target T6.0 atau 4.2 enchanted (IP ±950-1.050). Beli 2 set (1 pakai + 1 cadangan).
  • Mepet? Turunin ke T5/4.1 (IP ±850-950) — tetap bisa solo Ice Spider asal main sabar (kiting + lifesteal).
  • Sisihkan ±400k buat pot, food, dan mount. Jangan habisin semua di gear.
  • Lemah vs purge/silence (sustain mati). RoA mob aman, tapi waspada gank player.
